FEATURES

Everything a Leekha table needs, nothing it doesn't.

Built to be used mid-game: big tap targets, instant results and a calm, card-table look.

36

Auto-calculated scores

Enter three scores and leave one blank. The app works out the fourth so every round adds up to exactly 36 points.

♠

Live 52-card tracker

Tap a card as it is played. Played cards fade out and a counter shows how many are left in the deck.

+37

Double Leekha

One tap adds 37 points to the right player, ends the round and resets the card tracker.

★

Leader highlight

The player with the lowest score gets a star, since in Leekha lower is better.

✎

Round history

Review the most recent rounds at a glance, with penalty points highlighted in red.

↻

Quick new game

Start fresh and either reuse the previous player names or enter new ones.

HOW IT WORKS

Simple rules, built in.

Each round distributes 36 penalty points between the players. Leekhne enforces that total, flags impossible entries and keeps a running tally so nobody has to do mental maths mid-hand.
