✨ Features
Auto-calculated scores: leave one player's score blank and the app works it out so each round totals 36.
52-card tracker: tap cards as they are played and see how many remain.
Double Leekha: adds 37 points to the chosen player, ends the round and resets the deck.
Leader highlight: the lowest score gets a star.
Round history: the latest rounds at a glance, penalties shown in red.
Quick new game: reuse previous names or enter new ones.
Landscape mode: optional, from Settings.
